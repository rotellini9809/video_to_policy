



## E2 — Shuffle / Angle (Step–Intercept) [BACKUP]

### Purpose
**Get in the right place** (positioning / footwork). The ball is still far enough that you can improve coverage by moving your base. Success is measured by reducing positional error to where the shot will pass (predicted crossing point / corridor). Train **compact** locomotion while staying square (don’t collapse posture, don’t overrun, don’t leave the GK box).

### Reset distribution
- **Keeper (biased to Set & Square states):** start in a ready stance inside GK area with small pose/velocity noise, but with a designed lateral error.
  - Sample a privileged corridor target y_hit (see below) and set keeper_y = y_hit + offset.
  - Offset forces 1–3 shuffles (example magnitude: ~0.4–1.2 m, random sign).
- **Ball:** early iteration uses **ground shots** (low z, ~0 v_z) at moderate speed.
  - Default: **immediate launch at t=0** (moving shot).

### Environment mechanics (keep E2 from becoming E3)
- Recommended: **ghost / non-colliding ball** (passes through the keeper).
  - Prevents E2 from being solved by early bracing/blocking.
- Terminate based on geometry, not contact:
  - End when the ball reaches a **reference plane** (e.g., keeper-plane x_ref = GOAL_X − GK_MARGIN − 0.5 m) or when it enters the danger zone.
- Randomize speed/spawn/friction within moderate ranges, but keep elevation low (no high shots yet).

### Privileged signal (pick one)
- Predicted crossing point y_hit (and possibly t_ref) at the chosen reference plane
  OR
- An explicit intercept target marker inside the GK area

### Horizon
- **Max horizon (T_max): 2.0 s**
- Typically ends earlier when the ball reaches x_ref / enters danger zone.

### Reward
- Primary: reduce |keeper_y − y_hit| quickly (progress-based recommended)
- + yaw alignment
- + upright / stability
- − leave GK area
- − excessive forward/back motion (keep it mostly lateral)
- − large speed / overstepping penalty (keeps motion compact)

### Termination
- Ball reaches the reference plane OR enters danger zone
- Timeout
